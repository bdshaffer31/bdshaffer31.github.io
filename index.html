I am a PhD student in Mechnaical Engineering and Applied Mechanics at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Nat Trask and M. Ani Hsieh.

I develop geometric and structure-preserving machine learning methods for engineering. My research utilizes differential geometry, exterior calculus, and numerical PDEs to build learned models that generalize across complex geometries while preserving physical and mathematical structure.

Previously, I was a captain and research engineer at the Air Force Research Laboratory, working on numerical simulation and learning-based prediction for aero-optics.

Research

My research centers on structure-preserving learning for physical systems. Recent directions include:

  • Learning physics across geometries: meshfree exterior calculus and learned finite element models for PDEs on general domains (NeurIPS 2026, ICML 2026).
  • Reduced simulation and digital twins: learned finite element spaces and constitutive models with numerical structure (CNWF).
  • Physics-based robotics: incorporating learned physical models into autonomous sensing and source localization (ICRA 2026, preprint).
